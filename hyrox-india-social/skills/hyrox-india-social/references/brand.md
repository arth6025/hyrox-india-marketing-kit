# HYROX brand rules for social

Sources: *HYROX Brand Guidelines (update 12.2024)*, *HYROX Social Templates Local* (PDF, 65 pages),
and the HQ marketing links doc. Where these and this file disagree, HQ's documents win.

## The one rule

**Make it yours. Keep it HYROX.** (Templates PDF, "System rules")

| You can | Please don't |
|---|---|
| Replace copy | Move locked elements |
| Swap imagery | Change fonts, colours or layouts |
| Choose from available text styles | Create new layouts within the templates |
| Combine templates as needed | |

Templates must only be used for their specific purpose. Text sizes, colours and template structures stay
unchanged. Designated spaces stay what they are (a city-logo space is never replaced by an image, and vice versa).
**Missing a template? Don't build a new layout yourself.** Check the library → identify what's missing →
submit a design request on Monday. *Use the system. Don't rebuild the system.*

## Build a post in 3 steps

1. **Pick a cover:** World (curated, editorial, elite) or Local (event-led, race-driven, authentic).
2. **Add content:** as many content slides as the story needs.
3. **Add a finisher:** close with a CTA when needed.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#000000` | Base colour of nearly all graphics |
| Dark grey | `#313335` | Special cases only (HQ-approved) |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text, highlight boxes, logo on dark |
| HYROX Yellow | `#FFED00` | **Highlight box only**, never text colour |

- Text is predominantly white or black.
- Logos are only ever black or white.
- The purple gradient in the templates belongs to the **Pain Inc.** campaign (`"ground": "pain-inc"`); don't use it elsewhere.

## Typography

**Brutal Type**: Regular (default), **Medium** (the "highlighter" in headlines/body), Bold (only on yellow/white
grounds for legibility). Nothing else.

- Headlines: caps, Medium, tight leading.
- Text blocks: left-aligned by default.
- Single-digit numbers: **01, 02, …**
- **HYROX** always written in capitals.
- Highlighter boxes: text set on a white (or yellow) box, black text, e.g. `SUBHEADING`, `FOUNDERS NAME`, stat labels.
- Pills: small caps label in an outlined box, top-left (event name, campaign, "DON'T MISS OUT", "GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP").

Brutal Type is licensed. The skill uses it when installed, else **Barlow** (free) as the stand-in.

## Logos

- **Logotype first** (`H Y R O X`). The mark only supports it, and only appears alone where the brand is already clear (avatar, at the event, merch).
- Never stack the logotype and mark together, never outline, add transparency, recolour, distort or add text to them.
- Exclusion zone: left/right = 1 × the height of "O"; top/bottom = 0.75 × "O" height.
- **City logos** for event-specific comms ("HYROX GUJARAT", with a presenting partner when there is one). New city logos only come from HQ.
- **Claim logo** ("The World Series of Fitness Racing") for top-of-funnel comms.
- Use SVG when available; PNG is fine otherwise. Check fill/fit when swapping logos.

## Photography

| | World | Local |
|---|---|---|
| Feel | Curated · editorial · elite | Event-led · race-driven · authentic |
| What | Expressive images, tighter more artistic crops, elite athletes, monumental stages | Strong images from local races: active participation, true effort, community builders |

- Check the image fit after swapping: adjust the crop so the subject sits right (fill / fit / crop).
- **Picture dimming**: when text lacks contrast, dim the photo, *only as much as needed*. Keep the background visible and impactful.
- Bi-monthly "BEST OF" folders (HQ) hold free-to-use race photos for markets short on material (respect local partnerships).

## Voice

HYROX is the sport of fitness racing: authentic, open to every body, motivating, engaging, personal.
"HYROX IS FOR EVERYBODY." Short, confident lines: *RACE WITH YOUR FRIENDS. BE AMONG THE BESTS. TIME FOR RECAP.*

## Special cases

- **Workout of the week**: replace @HYROXWORLD with the local Instagram handle.
- **Elite 15 quotes**: Elite 15 athletes only. Other athletes (even major ones) use the standard quote template.
