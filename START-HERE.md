# Start here (no tech skills needed)

This kit lets you type what you want in plain English and get finished HYROX Instagram posts back.
You don't need to code, use Terminal, or unzip anything yourself. Claude does the setup for you.

## Step 1: Get the Claude app (one time)

1. Download the **Claude desktop app** from **claude.ai/download** and sign in.
2. Make sure **Google Chrome** is installed on your computer (Claude uses it to draw the posts).

## Step 2: Let Claude install the kit (one time, about 5 minutes)

1. Open the Claude app and click the **Code** tab at the top.
2. Pick any folder when it asks (for example, your Desktop).
3. Copy this sentence, paste it in, and press Enter:

> Install the HYROX India kit from https://github.com/arth6025/hyrox-india-marketing-kit for me. Download it, set up the HYROX India skill and the Marketing Kit so you can use them, and tell me in simple words when it's ready.

4. If Claude asks for permission to run something, click **Allow**.
5. When it says it's ready, **close the app and open it again**.

That's it. You never need to do this again.

## Step 3: Make your first post

Open the **Code** tab, start your message with **HYROX**, and say what you want. Drag your race photo into the chat if you have one.

**Copy any of these and change the words in [brackets]:**

> HYROX post. Headline: [RACE WITH YOUR FRIENDS]. Small line: [HYROX RELAYS >>]. Use the photo I attached.

> HYROX carousel with 4 slides. Cover: [8 STATIONS. 8 KM. ONE RACE.] Then explain the race, then [6,512 participants last season], then end with [REGISTER NOW].

> HYROX story set for [race day in Mumbai]: a cover, what's inside, the schedule, and a sign-up slide.

> HYROX partner post for [Myprotein], [Global Partner]. Their logo is attached.

> Write 10 catchy hooks for [HYROX Mumbai registrations opening], then make the best one a HYROX post.

Claude shows you the finished images. They're also saved in your folder, ready to post.

## Changing a post

Just reply in plain words, like you'd tell a designer:

- "Make the headline shorter"
- "Use the other photo"
- "Put the headline in the middle"
- "Make a story version too"

If Claude says **"copy too long"**, cut a few words. HYROX's templates keep text sizes fixed on purpose.
